Internet of the Future

    The internet started out as a small way of connecting to the people around us. But now, the reach of the internet reached a global scale that jumpstarted companies to take business and money seriously within the scope of a single screen. How I envision the internet of the future based on current trends is the fact that due to the economic crisis that we are going through, its going to be filled with A.I. focused trends that support the mainframe of each and every single corner of the internet. While we are still living in an era where private corporations are still focused on brand deals and recognition, The overall cheapness in the usage of A.I. in exchange for human labor may shift for their bottom line.

    Within the landscape of social media, you have people who use youtube, instagram, twitter and even twitch to chase trends and even carve out their own niche to gain a following online. What used be a platform for broadcasting ones own personality and having fun with it became moreso a breeding ground for who would stand on the top of the list. Over the years, social media platforms have only encouraged and pushed influencers who farm engagement on their platforms in order to keep the online traffic constantly rolling in. Pewdiepie, a swedish youtuber known for his funny reactions while doing gameplay videos and crazy antics landed him at the top of the Youtube charts. In 2019, the bollywood company T-Series was rising up the charts as the company gained ground as more people in India were getting access to the internet enmasse. Because Youtube cared more about the corporate brand image of the company, Pewdiepie did not want any part in promoting himself as a brand for Youtube to flourish. In the end, Youtube fully embraced the corporate image by allowing T-Series to take up the head of the youtube market. This was during a time where people still cared about what the people put out rather than what a brand and existing brands can do for Youtube.

People that have only seen what you post online will develop this idea of knowing who you are. This is what can be considered to be parasocial tendencies. Parasocial Tendencies pertain to developing a relationship with a persona that you put out online for people to entertain themselves with and even feel strongly towards. Many big names have contributed to the parasocial aspect of the internet community but I would say the ones who made the biggest splash were the rise of Vtubers during the start of the pandemic in 2020. Vtubers make up the social media landscape that took an anime girl and added a personality to accompany the character’s look.
