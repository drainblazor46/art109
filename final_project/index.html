Remembering Hi-Fi Rush

Introduction

Hi-Fi Rush is a video game that blends the action and rhythm genre into a wonderful mix of beating up enemies to the beat. Get strung along with the strumming of the guitar strings, wonderful vocals, and the music that ties into the heartbeat of the world. Team up with Chai and many other allies that you will meet to face each and every corporate higher up to confront the realities of a hostile takeover of a company.
