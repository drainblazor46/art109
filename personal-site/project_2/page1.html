The Neighbors

Entry 1

Ever since my parents passed away, a series of illnesses have befallen me.

It all began with physical ailments like coughing and sneezing.

It didn't seem so bad at first until it led to something worse.

I could not stop crying my eyes out. I went to the doctors and they couldn't find ways to treat it either.

I've been crying a river and my eyes are blood shot red.

Help Me.
